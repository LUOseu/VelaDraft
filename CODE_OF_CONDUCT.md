# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant. Be respectful and
constructive; assume good faith; focus critiques on code, claims and evidence,
never on people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory language, and
publishing others' private information. Maintainers may remove comments,
commits and issues that violate this policy, and may block accounts for
repeated violations.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` for private
contact). Reports are handled confidentially.
